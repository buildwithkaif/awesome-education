About Us

We Are More Than Just a
Service Provider

Awesome Education partners with schools to create measurable growth beyond textbooks. Our ecosystem connects students, parents, teachers and leadership through programs that build achievement, exposure, confidence and community.

Founded by Awesome Ideas & Events Pvt. Ltd., Awesome Education began with a simple belief: schools deserve a partner that can execute meaningful academic and co-curricular growth programs with professionalism, warmth and measurable outcomes.
Our mission is to help every school deliver holistic growth through competitive learning, experiential exposure, teacher enablement, parent involvement and clear performance insights.
Our vision is to become India's most trusted school growth ecosystem, where every learner discovers ability, every teacher feels empowered and every school builds a culture of real progress.
