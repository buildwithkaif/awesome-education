National Science Olympiad 2025

The National Science Olympiad is a prestigious competition designed to identify and nurture scientific talent among students across India. Participants will compete in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, with questions testing conceptual understanding, analytical skills, and practical application. Top performers will receive recognition, certificates, and scholarships for advanced scientific studies.

Location: Delhi, India
Time: 9:00 AM onwards
Participants: 500+ Students
